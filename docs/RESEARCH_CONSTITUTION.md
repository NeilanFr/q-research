# Research constitution

COMPETITION_V1 / ridge10 (`cbe43010220707651123`) remains champion. Its frozen
source, parameters, historical records and PAPER permissions are preserved.
V2 is research and shadow infrastructure only. It cannot send broker orders.

The required sequence is raw point-in-time data, causal features, preregistration,
discovery, nested purged walk-forward evaluation, multiplicity and robustness
audit, immutable candidate freeze, prospective shadow/PAPER observations, and a
separate promotion decision. Every gate must pass. No historical statistic alone
can promote a strategy. Missing evidence is a failed gate, never a presumed pass.

The current data fails the first gate: it is a 2026-vintage revised-price panel
with incomplete historical identities and corporate-action lineage. V2 may run
explicitly labelled exploratory falsification experiments to reject hypotheses;
it cannot convert these experiments into point-in-time evidence or freeze a
promotable challenger. Data repairs require a new preregistered study.

The 2023-01-01 through 2026-09-18 holdout was permanently opened on September 21,
2026. The opening is recorded in `config/research_history.json`; old archived
claims of a sealed holdout describe an earlier state. Reused 2018-2022 validation
is also not independent confirmation. Neither interval may be resealed.

`config/rigorous_v2.json` specifies 20 challengers, eight baselines, four negative
controls, a 2,500-simulation ceiling, three counted operational attempts and fixed
acceptance rules. No data-dependent parameter expansion is allowed. Perturbations
are predeclared fragility checks, never selectable replacements. Every started
trial, failure, exclusion and result is appended to `research/v2_ledger.jsonl`.
The hash chain and Git commits expose edits; they are not trusted timestamping or
protection against an owner rewriting the entire repository.

No strategy is called causal alpha merely because its code is temporally causal.
Confounding, revised observations, selection and economic execution remain
separate questions. Negative conclusions and missing evidence are first-class
research results.
